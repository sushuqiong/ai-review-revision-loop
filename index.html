📋 AI Review-Revision Loop

A systematic loop for reviewing medical manuscripts with AI reviewers (simulating high-impact journal editors), then fixing issues by priority — evidence-based, reproducible, privacy-aware.

Academic writingPeer-review simulationEvidence-based

Why this skill exists

Writing a manuscript is only half the battle — passing peer review is the other half. This skill encodes a proven workflow: use AI reviewers that actually read your manuscript and verify numbers against your analysis outputs, then fix issues by severity. It was refined through real Major Revision cycles on three medical papers (epidemiology, mediation analysis, prediction-model development).

The loop

1
AI peer review
One reviewer subagent per paper, reading the manuscript (python-docx), cross-checking every number against RDS/CSV outputs, and verifying references via PubMed — no fabricated opinions.
2
Priority-based fixing
P0 data-integrity (impossible values, internal contradictions) → P1 methodology (reproducibility, missing comparators) → P2 formatting (citations, figures) → P3 language.
3
Honest recalculation
Every new number (E-value, quartile ORs, DCA) is recomputed in R — never invented.
4
Submission packaging
7-folder delivery structure with vector figures (SVG/PDF/PNG), cover letters, and graphical abstracts.

Key pitfalls documented

  • ⚠️ DCA net benefit cannot exceed outcome prevalence — impossible values are data-integrity red flags
  • ⚠️ Recalibrated probabilities must not be used to assess calibration (circularity)
  • ⚠️ Prediction-model papers must report the full formula (z-score constants + intercept + lambda)
  • ⚠️ Vancouver citations must follow first-appearance order — including citations inside tables and figure captions
  • ⚠️ Same-source time-window validation ≠ true external validation (use "temporal validation")
  • ⚠️ Shared-component mathematical coupling between exposure and outcome must be discussed
  • ⚠️ Privacy: never publish user names, emails, or institution paths in public skill repos
